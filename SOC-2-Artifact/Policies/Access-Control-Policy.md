# TacoTrax Access Control Policy
**Effective Date:** September 17, 2026  
**Scope:** All TacoTrax production environments, cloud systems, and internal tooling.

## 1. Purpose
This policy defines the requirements for managing access to TacoTrax systems and customer data to ensure confidentiality, integrity, and availability.

## 2. Authentication & MFA
- Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) is strictly enforced for all employees, contractors, and administrators accessing production software and cloud resources.
- Passwords must meet complexity requirements and be managed via approved password managers.

## 3. Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
- Access is granted based on the principle of least privilege according to employee job roles.
- Access reviews are conducted quarterly to remove deprecated permissions.
